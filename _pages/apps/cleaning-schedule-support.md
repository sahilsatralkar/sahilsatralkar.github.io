## Requirements

- An iPhone running iOS 26 or later
- Notification permission for reminders and assignment notices
- An available Apple Account and iCloud for cloud synchronization
- An available Apple Account and iCloud for household sharing
- Active Premium access for protected household-collaboration actions

Local household planning can remain available without iCloud.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my household data stored?

Cleaning Schedule can store a household locally on the iPhone or in your Apple iCloud account.

A household you own may synchronize through your private CloudKit database. A household shared by another person appears through Apple's shared CloudKit database after you accept the invitation.

Cleaning Schedule does not operate a separate household-data server.

### Is iCloud synchronization immediate?

No. CloudKit synchronization is managed by Apple and is asynchronous.

Changes normally synchronize automatically, but they may be delayed by the network, iCloud availability, device state, or Apple service conditions. Cleaning Schedule does not include a manual sync button or claim an exact last-synchronized time.

### What is the difference between Apple Family Sharing and household sharing?

They are separate features.

Apple Family Sharing may provide eligible subscription access to family members.

Cleaning Schedule household sharing sends a CloudKit invitation that provides access to household data.

Receiving one does not automatically provide the other. Every participant needs active Premium access from their own subscription or an eligible Family-shared subscription before protected collaboration actions are available.

### Why is a shared household read-only?

A shared household may become read-only when:

- Premium access is inactive;
- Premium access can no longer be verified;
- the Apple Account or iCloud identity is unavailable;
- an accepted participant profile is still being created;
- household deletion is pending; or
- household membership has ended.

The app preserves data when possible and shows the relevant recovery action.

### I accepted an invitation but cannot edit anything. What should I do?

Confirm that:

1. the invitation was opened using the intended Apple Account;
2. iCloud and iCloud Drive are available;
3. the iPhone has a network connection;
4. Cleaning Schedule shows active Premium access; and
5. the app has completed creation of your household member profile.

Apple Family Sharing access can take time to appear. Use **Restore Purchases** if an eligible purchase or Family-shared subscription is not reflected.

### Why did my task change appear later on another iPhone?

CloudKit synchronizes changes asynchronously. A change saved on one iPhone may take time to arrive on another.

Keep both devices connected to the network with iCloud available. Cleaning Schedule retries eligible synchronization work automatically.

### Why did I not receive a reminder?

Check **Settings → Notifications** inside Cleaning Schedule.

If notification access is disabled, use **Open iOS Settings** and allow notifications for Cleaning Schedule.

Notification delivery also depends on iOS scheduling and device conditions. Notifications are not guaranteed, and the task list inside the app remains the primary record.

A reminder may also be removed after a task is completed, cancelled, covered by a household vacation, removed from the household, or made ineligible by membership or Premium changes.

### How do I restore Premium access?

Open **Settings → Subscription** and choose **Restore Purchases**.

Use the Apple Account associated with the original purchase or eligible Family Sharing access.

If support is required, copy the **Purchase Support ID** from the same screen and include it in your email. Do not send an App Store receipt unless Apple Support specifically requests it.

### How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Open **Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription** in Cleaning Schedule.

The app opens Apple's subscription-management interface.

You can also follow Apple's instructions at [If you want to cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/118428).

Deleting Cleaning Schedule does not cancel a subscription.

### How do I request a refund?

Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests.

Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) and follow Apple's instructions. Cleaning Schedule support cannot directly approve an App Store refund.

### What happens when Premium expires?

A solo household follows the Free-plan limits.

A shared household is preserved but protected collaboration becomes read-only for the participant whose Premium access is inactive. Restoring eligible Premium access can re-enable collaboration without creating a new household share.

### How do I delete my household data?

If you own the household, use **Delete Household** in the Household section of Cleaning Schedule Settings.

Confirmed deletion:

- removes the household graph;
- ends its CloudKit share;
- removes participant access; and
- has no app-provided undo or restoration.

Cloud-backed deletion may remain pending until iCloud confirms the server change.

If you are a household member rather than the owner, use **Leave Household** to end participation.

Deleting the app alone does not delete household data from iCloud or end participation in a household share.

### How do I delete local app data?

Deleting Cleaning Schedule removes its local app container and device-local preferences.

This does not automatically:

- delete cloud-backed household data;
- end a household share;
- cancel a subscription; or
- delete purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat.

Use the appropriate **Leave Household**, **Delete Household**, or Apple subscription-management action first.

### Does Cleaning Schedule track me?

Cleaning Schedule does not contain advertising, cross-app tracking, or custom product-usage analytics.

RevenueCat processes the anonymous purchase and entitlement information required for subscriptions. Apple may provide aggregate App Store and diagnostic information according to your Apple privacy settings.

See the [Cleaning Schedule Privacy Policy](https://sahilsatralkar.com/apps/cleaning-schedule/privacy) for details.
