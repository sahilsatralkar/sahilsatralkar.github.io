## What TidyTempo does

TidyTempo helps people in one household plan, assign, and track one-time and repeating cleaning tasks.

The app can store a household on an iPhone, synchronize a household between devices through iCloud and, when eligible, share a household with invited participants through Apple CloudKit.

TidyTempo does not provide a separate developer-operated user account or authentication system. Apple Account, iCloud, App Store subscription, and household membership are separate services and states.
